Player statistics

Player statistics have been compiled using a combination of partial CricHQ data recovered by NZC and PlayHQ data (2022/23–present).

The recovered CricHQ data includes matches that were scored live, but does not include manually uploaded scorecards, which were used until 2019/20. As a result, many B Grade and Presidents’ fixtures from that period are not yet available.

As a result, some performances recorded on the Honours Board may not yet appear in individual player statistics, particularly where matches were not scored live and the original scorecards have not been recovered.

We are actively working to recover and compile historical statistics, with the aim of making our all-time player statistics available for everyone to enjoy.

Watch this space!

Statistics currently available:

  • Senior A: 2015/16–present
  • Senior B: 2018/19–present
  • Presidents: 2019/20–present (no 2021/22 data)
Built from the club’s stats spreadsheets and honours board. A grade stats cover 2015/16 onwards; Senior B covers 2018/19 onwards; Presidents covers 2019/20 onwards (no 2021/22 data). The 2026/27 season is in progress.
